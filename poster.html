What Changed & Why

Voter Privacy & Safeguards — built in, not bolted on

✓
No voter photography, ever — registration and election-day flows use verified ID lookup and canvass notes only. No image of any voter is captured at any point.
✓
No individual geolocation — the app records which polling unit a canvasser is assigned to, never a GPS coordinate tied to a specific voter's movements.
✓
No pay-per-voter compensation — field staff recognition is based on contact volume only; nothing in the app calculates or triggers a payout tied to an individual's turnout.
✓
Aggregate reporting to HQ — the situation room sees polling-unit and zone-level rollups (e.g. "62% of assigned list contacted"), never a per-voter turnout record.
✓
Self-reported GOTV status — "voted" flags on election day come from the voter's own word to the canvasser, the same way any door-knock or phone-bank GOTV program works — not from evidentiary capture.
✓
NDPR-aligned by design — the data model was scoped down to what a field-ops and GOTV tool actually needs, removing the categories of data that carry the highest privacy and coercion risk.

Core Features, Specifications & Data Model

Core Features

  • 1 Supervisor + 10 Canvassers per polling unit
  • Verified voter list lookup by polling unit
  • Voter engagement (pre-election) canvass logging
  • GOTV Tracker for election-day reminders & follow-up
  • 3-contact engagement target per canvasser
  • Team management & real-time overview
  • Offline-first — works without internet
  • Secure sync & encrypted backup
  • Reports, analytics & dashboards
  • Incidents & alerts
  • Role-based access & permissions

App Specifications

  • Platform: Android 8.0 and above
  • Devices: 176,974 tablets
  • Connectivity: 2G / 3G / 4G / Wi-Fi
  • Offline storage: Encrypted SQLite
  • Sync: Event-based, auto / manual
  • Auth: PIN / password + device certificate
  • Security: AES-256 encryption, TLS 1.3
  • Camera: Optional — issue/incident evidence only
  • Languages: English, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Pidgin
  • App size: ~40MB
  • Update mode: Silent updates via MDM

Data Capture Model

Pre-Election (Canvass)
  • Voter ID (verified against roll)
  • Polling unit, date & time
  • Canvass result & notes
Election Day (GOTV Tracker)
  • Self-reported voting status
  • Date & time of update
  • Canvasser ID (for audit only)
Explicitly Not Captured
  • Voter photographs, at any stage
  • Per-voter GPS / geolocation
  • Any payment or payout data
